\n\nNameless Honor is a recurring Battle Pass event in Honkai: Star Rail. It is unlocked after the completion of Trailblaze Mission Long Wait for the Blade's Edge. Once unlocked, players can complete various weekly and \"This Period\" missions in order to gain The Nameless EXP.\n

Each level requires The Nameless EXP ×800.[Note 1] Upon reaching each of the 70 levels, players will unlock rewards depending on their Battle Pass type (Nameless Gift or Nameless Glory). Stellar Jade ×150 can be used to purchase a level.\n

Nameless Honor is the thirty-first Battle Pass.\n

\n
\n

Nameless Gift[]

\n

Nameless Gift is unlocked by default at the start of each Nameless Honor Period. It awards the following items after reaching the appropriate level in Nameless Honor:\n

\n
5 Star Rail Pass
2 Tracks of Destiny
2 Self-Modeling Resin
6 Fuel
42 Traveler's Guide
42 Adventure Log
42 Refined Aether
30 Condensed Aether
510,000 Credit
\n

Nameless Glory[]

\n

Nameless Glory can be purchased for $9.99 USD and will unlock a second tier for the current Nameless Honor Period, as well as grant Stellar Jade ×680. It includes an extra reward at Nameless Honor Lv. 30 where the player can choose one of the Light Cones from Jewels of the Starry Seas.\n

Purchasing Nameless Glory awards the following items in addition to those obtained in Nameless Gift after reaching the appropriate level in Nameless Honor:\n

\n
680 Stellar Jade
1 Light Cone Memory Shard
4 Star Rail Special Pass
2 Tracks of Destiny
2 Self-Modeling Resin
1 Variable Dice
600 Relic Remains
20 Lost Crystal
1 Jewels of the Starry Seas
312 Tears of Dreams
6 Fuel
210 Traveler's Guide
210 Refined Aether
1,240,000 Credit
\n

Nameless Medal[]

\n

Nameless Medal can be purchased for $19.99 USD (or $11.99 if the player has already purchased Nameless Glory). It will advance Nameless Honor by 10 levels, unlock Nameless Medal Profile Pictures exclusive to the current Nameless Honor Period, and award Stellar Jade ×200 and Fuel ×5.\n

\n
200 Stellar Jade
1 Light Cone Memory Shard
5 Fuel
1 Ashveil: Gallop
1 Jade: Gallop
