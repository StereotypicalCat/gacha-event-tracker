How to Redeem Codes

Redeem From the In-Game Menu

Codes can be entered into the Redeem Code option in the game's main menu.

  • Log into the game.
  • Tap Menu in the top right-hand corner of the screen.
  • Tap the [...] button.
  • Select Redeem Code.
  • Enter the code.

Can Only Be Redeemed In-Game

As there is currently no redemption portal on the official website, codes must be redeemed in-game.
