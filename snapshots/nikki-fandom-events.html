Types of Events[]

\n

Events can be time-limited or permanent, and can be categorized into one of the following types.\n

\n\n\n\n\n\n\n\n\n\n\n\n\n\n\n\n\n\n\n\n\n\n\n\n\n
Type of EventDescription\n
QuestPlayers can complete the event-exclusive quests to unlock event-exclusive rewards.\n
TaskPlayers can complete the event-exclusive tasks to unlock event-exclusive rewards.\n
Check-inPlayers can unlock event-exclusive rewards by checking in.\n
Double RewardsPlayers can get double rewards in Realm Challenges.\n
WebPlayers can participate in web events to earn event-exclusive rewards.\n
StorePlayers can purchase items in the Store to obtain event-exclusive rewards.\n
RecurringEvents that have more than one run.\n
