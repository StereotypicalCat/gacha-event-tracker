\n\nThe New Eridu City Fund is a Battle Pass in Zenless Zone Zero. Players can complete various daily, weekly and Season Tasks in order to gain City Fund EXP.\n

Each of the 50 levels in the New Eridu City Fund requires 1,000 EXP. Upon reaching each level, players will unlock rewards depending on their Plan type (Basic Plan, Growth Plan, or Premium Plan). Polychrome ×150 can also be used to purchase level(s).\n

Event Details[]

\n

Duration[]

\n
  • After version update, around 10:00:00 (GMT+8) — two days before the next version update, 03:59:59 (server time)
    • \n
  • Purchasing of the Growth Plan, Premium Plan, and Plan Upgrade for the current event will close at 02:59:59 (server time) of the cutoff day, therefore one hour before the event ends. Once purchasing is closed, Proxies may still complete Expansion Tasks and claim rewards. Please pay attention to purchase availability times and the event duration.
\n

Requirements[]

\n
  • Unlock Combat Simulation in Main Story Chapter 1 \"Cat's Lost & Found.\"
\n

Overview[]

\n
  • At the start of each season of the New Eridu City Fund, the Basic Plan is automatically unlocked. Proxies can complete Expansion Tasks for experience and increase their Fund Level to obtain more rewards.
    • \n
  • Proxies who have already unlocked the \"Growth Plan\" can purchase the \"Plan Upgrade\" to upgrade to the \"Premium Plan.\"
    • \n
  • After unlocking the Growth Plan or Premium Plan and reaching City Fund Level Lv. 30, Proxies can claim the Great Builder's Certificate. The Great Builder's Certificate is a time-limited reward for the current City Fund period and will not be retained for the next New Eridu City fund period. It will expire automatically if not used within the time limit.
\n

Choose Rewards[]

\n

From Version 2.5, upon opening the New Eridu City Fund, the player can choose to boost several of the reward items. There are a set of Base Rewards, from which the player can choose 3 to boost, and a set of Advanced Rewards, from which the player can select only 1 to boost. Depending on the items chosen to boost, the Basic Plan and Growth Plan reward tracks will change accordingly.\n

\n

    • \n
  • Drive Disc EXP Material

\n

The player can pick any 1 of the following to boost:\n

\n
