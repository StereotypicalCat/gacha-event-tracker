Miliastra Pass is a recurring Event for Miliastra Wonderland. It is a Battle Pass that uses Chronicle EXP gained from weekly and BP period quests to reward Geodes of Replication, Cosmetics, Interactive Expressions, Lustrous Materials, Lobby Facilities, and Chronicle Caches. It includes the Chronicles of the Sky (basic edition), Chronicles of the Realms, and Epic of the Realms.\n

Overview[]

\n

The Miliastra Pass contains a total of 30 Chronicle Levels, with each level up requiring 1,000 Chronicle EXP. Upon reaching each level, players will unlock rewards depending on their Miliastra Pass type. Chronal Nexus ×150 can be used to purchase a level and to level up immediately.\n

There is a weekly Chronicle EXP cap of 5,000, which resets on 4 AM server time each Monday. The pass period missions do not count towards the EXP cap.\n

Purchasing the Chronicles of the Realms or Epic of the Realms packages allows the player to claim rewards from all previously unlocked levels in the current Miliastra Pass period. These packages must be re-purchased every new pass period.\n

\n

Boon[]

\n

After unlocking \"Chronicles of the Realms\" or \"Epic of the Realms,\" the player will unlock the Battle Pass Boon effect, granting a discount when purchasing the corresponding Battle Pass in Teyvat. Likewise, unlocking \"Gnostic Hymn\" or \"Gnostic Chorus\" will grant the Chronicle Pass Boon effect, offering a discount when purchasing the corresponding Miliastra Pass in Miliastra Wonderland. The player can check the Boon button on the Miliastra Pass or Battle Pass purchase screen for more details. The Boon effect can only be activated during the current event period.\n

\n

Chronicles of the Realms[]

\n

Chronicles of the Realms can be purchased for $5.00 USD and will unlock a second tier for the current BP Period, containing enhanced general rewards and Star Tokens.\n

\n

Epic of the Realms[]

\n

Epic of the Realms can be purchased for $14.99 USD (or $9.99 USD if the player has already purchased Chronicles of the Realms). It will advance the Chronicle Level by 5, and contains a Miliastra Pass exclusive Collection Template.\n
